Dog Door Installation for Pool Cages

Give your pets the freedom they deserve with our professional dog door installation for pool cages. This service allows your dog to safely move in and out of your screened enclosure without damaging the structure or screens.

We custom-build a reinforced frame and install a durable pet door directly into your pool cage, ensuring a clean, secure, and long-lasting solution.

What Does Dog Door Installation Include?

Installing a dog door in a pool cage is not just cutting the screen. It requires proper framing and reinforcement to maintain the strength and appearance of the enclosure.

Our service includes:

✔ Custom aluminum framing for structural support
✔ Precise screen cutting and clean finishing
✔ Installation of a high-quality pet door
✔ Secure mounting to prevent sagging or damage
✔ Seamless integration with your existing enclosure

Why Proper Framing Matters

Without proper framing, cutting into the screen can weaken your pool cage and cause tears or instability over time.

Our reinforced framing:

✔ Maintains the integrity of the structure
✔ Prevents screen damage and sagging
✔ Provides a solid base for the pet door
✔ Ensures a professional, clean look

Benefits of a Pool Cage Dog Door

✔ Easy access for your pets anytime
✔ No more scratching or damaging screens
✔ Improves convenience for pet owners
✔ Safe and controlled outdoor access
✔ Adds functionality to your enclosure

Custom Fit for Any Enclosure

We install dog doors for all types of pool cages and screen enclosures. Each installation is customized based on your pet’s size and your enclosure’s design to ensure the perfect fit.

Clean, Professional Installation

Our team focuses on precision and quality, making sure your dog door looks like it was always part of your pool cage. No loose edges, no weak points—just a strong, clean finish.
