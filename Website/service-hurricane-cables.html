Hurricane cable installation

Engineered reinforcement that ties critical frame points together before the wind hits.

Protect your enclosure with cables designed for Florida’s wind loads. SCREEN ARMORS installs systems that link key structural members so your cage resists flex, uplift, and debris strikes far better than hardware alone.

Every run is tensioned and anchored with clean routing so performance doesn’t come at the expense of appearance. Don’t wait for a named storm to wish you’d reinforced—this upgrade is one of the smartest insurance policies you can buy for a pool cage.

Why cables matter

  • Greater stability when gusts hit broad screen walls
  • Less frame flex that can fatigue fasteners over time
  • Helps resist catastrophic failure in extreme events
  • Extends service life of the entire structure
  • Extra confidence heading into hurricane season

What we install

  • New cable layouts sized to your pool cage geometry
  • Reinforcement passes on existing builds
  • Replacement of corroded or overstretched cable runs
  • Storm-readiness inspections with upgrade options
  • Quality materials installed to Florida-minded standards
