Custom Aluminum Screen Enclosures

Professionally built aluminum screen enclosures for pools, patios, and lanais—designed for Florida weather, long-term durability, and a clean finish that complements your home.

From custom design and installation to structural upgrades, rescreening, and hardware replacement, we help you get more comfort and usability from your outdoor space.

Screening

Professional screen installation and replacement services for existing structures. Whether you need to replace damaged screens, upgrade to better materials, or install screens on a new frame, our team has the expertise to get the job done right.

We offer various screen types including standard fiberglass, pet-resistant screens, and solar screens. Our screening services ensure a tight, secure fit that keeps insects out while maintaining excellent airflow and visibility.

Refurbishing and Painting

Give your existing screen enclosure a new lease on life with our comprehensive refurbishing and painting services. We can restore aging structures to like-new condition, addressing frame repairs, screen replacement, hardware updates, and structural improvements.

Our refurbishing and painting process includes a thorough inspection, necessary repairs, and complete restoration. We can transform worn, damaged enclosures into beautiful, functional spaces that you'll enjoy for years to come.

Pressure Wash

Professional pool cage and screen enclosure washing built for Florida—soft-wash techniques that lift mold, algae, and grime without damaging mesh or frames.

Inspection, pre-treatment, controlled low-pressure cleaning, and detail work on corners and joints for a bright, streak-free finish.

Dog Door

Custom-framed pet door installation for pool cages and lanais—reinforced aluminum, precise screen work, and a durable flap so pets can come and go without tearing mesh or weakening the structure.

Sized for your dog and your enclosure, with secure mounting and a finish that looks built-in from day one.

Hurricane Cables

Professionally installed hurricane cables reinforce your pool cage against high winds and storms—strategic anchoring, proper tensioning, and materials suited to Florida weather.

New installs, replacements, upgrades for existing cages, and layouts tailored to your structure for stronger, longer-lasting protection.

Screw Replacement

Swap corroded, failing fasteners for rust-resistant hardware—restore strength at joints and plates, stop rust bleed on your frame, and help your enclosure hold up to Florida humidity and storm season.

Full inspection, careful removal, upgraded screws, and a final walkthrough so every connection is tight and secure.

Gutter Services

Protect your home with reliable gutter solutions built for Florida’s heavy rain—replacement, cleaning, leak sealing, and super gutter upgrades.

Durable results, improved water flow, and a clean, professional finish every time.
