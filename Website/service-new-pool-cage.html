New Pool Cage Construction & Extensions

Whether you're starting from scratch or expanding what you already have, we design and build it to look like it was always part of the house.

Serving Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, and Port Charlotte. See our full coverage list on Areas We Serve.

Build a brand-new pool cage designed for Florida living. At SCREEN ARMORS, we design and construct custom aluminum pool enclosures from the ground up, tailored to your home, layout, and outdoor lifestyle. From open panoramic designs to large screened lanais and patio enclosures, our team delivers durable structures engineered to withstand Florida weather while enhancing comfort, protection, and curb appeal.

In addition to new construction, we also provide existing pool cage extensions and structural additions. Whether you want to expand your current enclosure, add more covered space, extend around a new pool area, or connect an existing lanai to another section of your home, we can design seamless additions that blend naturally with your current structure.

Two paths—one accountable team

Pick the scope that fits your project. We handle the engineering, build, and finish from start to close-out.

  • Brand-new pool cages

    Custom aluminum pool enclosures designed from scratch around your home, pool layout, and views. Panoramic, picture-window, mansard, gable, or hip-style designs—engineered for Florida sun, rain, and humidity.

  • Pool cage extensions

    Expand your existing cage, add covered outdoor space, extend around a new pool area, or connect a lanai to another part of the home. Clean tie-ins that look original to the structure.

  • Replacement & tear-downs

    Replacing an old, storm-damaged, or undersized enclosure? We remove the old structure and build a stronger, better-looking cage in its place—upgraded fasteners, framing, and screen options included.
