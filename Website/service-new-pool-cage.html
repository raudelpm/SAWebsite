New Pool Cage Questions

Do you build new pool cages and extensions?

Yes. We build a new aluminum pool cage from the ground up, extend the cage you already have, and replace a cage when the old frame should come down.

What is the difference between a new cage, an extension, and a restoration?

A new cage is a full aluminum enclosure measured to the pool and the house. An extension adds onto the cage you already have. A restoration keeps a solid frame, replaces the hardware, paints the aluminum, and installs new screen. If the frame is bent, storm-damaged, or too small, we take it down and build a new one.

Can you add onto the cage I already have?

Yes. An extension can add covered space, wrap a new pool area, or connect the lanai to another part of the house. The new aluminum ties into the existing frame so the addition lines up with what is already there.

When should I rebuild instead of restore?

Restore the cage when the frame is still solid and the problems are screen, paint, or hardware. Rebuild when the frame is bent, storm-damaged, badly deteriorated, or too small for how you use the pool. We look at the structure and recommend the path that fits. Read the comparison.

What roof styles can you build?

Gable, mansard, hip, and a standard screen roof. The roof follows the house and the way you want the space to feel. A gable gives a peaked roof and more height. A mansard angles in and then flattens across the top. A hip slopes down on the sides. Compare roof shapes.

Can I get a Clear View pool cage?

Yes. A Clear View, also called a panoramic cage, uses wider screened openings and fewer posts in the view. The screen is polyester 16/14, or 17/20 no-see-um polyester. It can be one section or run across the walls.

What screen can I choose?

18/14 standard screen, 20/20 no-see-um mesh, 16/14 and 17/20 heavy-duty polyester, PetScreen, Florida Glass, 40/40 Shade Screen, and Super Screen. See the screens. The mesh is chosen for the openings that need it.

Do new cages include doors, kick plates, and hurricane cables?

A screen door is part of the build and goes on the opening you use. An aluminum kick plate, hurricane cables, and a pet door can be added. The cage is fastened with 304 marine-grade stainless steel screws.

Do I need a permit?

For new construction, permit requirements vary by city, size, and the type of enclosure. We tell you what applies to the project before work starts. An HOA review can also affect the start date.

How long does a new pool cage take?

A new cage takes longer than a repair. The schedule follows the size, the roof style, and any permit or HOA review. We confirm the timeline with the free estimate.

How much does a new pool cage cost?

The price follows the footprint, the roof, the screen, and whether the job is a new cage, an extension, or a tear-down and rebuild. Request a free estimate or call (941) 404-9699.

What warranty is included?

A 2-year warranty covers our workmanship. The screen we install carries a manufacturer warranty starting at 5 years.

Which areas do you serve?

Sarasota, Manatee, and Charlotte counties, including Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Lakewood Ranch. See the full list on Areas We Serve.
