Pool cage pressure washing

Deep clean without tearing screens—built for Florida grime and humidity.

Dirt, algae, mold, and mildew collect fast in Florida humidity. SCREEN ARMORS uses controlled pressure and enclosure-safe chemistry to lift buildup and brighten aluminum—without the damage risk of careless high-pressure blasting.

Residential or commercial pool cages, lanais, and patios: routine maintenance or a full refresh. We detail corners, joints, and transitions so your outdoor space feels inviting again.

Why wash on a schedule

  • Removes mold, mildew, algae, and embedded dirt
  • Restores a bright, like-new appearance
  • Helps prevent staining and long-term surface damage
  • Fresher air around the pool deck
  • Boosts curb appeal before guests or listing photos

Our cleaning process

  • Inspection — map stains, oxidation, and delicate areas
  • Pre-treatment — break down organic growth safely
  • Soft wash — low pressure that respects screens & paint
  • Detail pass — corners, fasteners, hard-to-reach lines
  • Final rinse — streak-free finish, ready to enjoy
