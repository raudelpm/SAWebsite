North Port sits between Sarasota County and Charlotte County. We cover it from Nokomis the same as Venice or Port Charlotte. HOA rules on some streets only affect paint color and mesh darkness—we work within those specs when you tell us the requirement.

Pool Cage Repair

On larger North Port cages, a storm may open three roof bays and leave the rest intact. Pool cage repair is those bays, not a new building. We also check mid-span fasteners; big roofs flex more than a small city lot cage.

Screen Replacement

Single panel screen repair is still the first visit when a ball, a pet, or a ladder punched one section. If the builder mesh is already chalking across a 20-year-old cage, we plan a full rescreen. Panel and full-cage screening are the same crew.

Lanai Screen Repair

Many North Port homes use the lanai as the main living porch. Lanai screen repair here is usually the door wall and the first course kids and dogs hit. We can step up to pet screen on those bays only.

Full Rescreening

Pool cage rescreening on a large footprint takes planning so the pool is not open for days. We stage walls, keep a path to the house, and finish with a walkthrough of doors and roof tension. Screen enclosure repair at this scale still costs less than replacing good aluminum.

Screen Materials

18/14 is standard on most North Port roofs. No-see-um is useful near canals and conservation edges. Pet screen on the lower course is the usual family upgrade. If the frame is dull or stained, restoration before new mesh keeps rust from telegraphing through.
