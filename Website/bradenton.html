Bradenton cages sit in a different climate than a dry inland lot. Afternoon storms push water through tired spline, and older west-side homes still have original mesh that has gone brittle. SCREEN ARMORS repairs those enclosures and will not sell a tear-down if the aluminum is still square.

Pool Cage Repair

On many Bradenton properties the first failure is a roof panel after limbs drop, or a door that no longer latches after the frame shifts. Pool cage repair here is usually that panel, the closer, and a fastener check—not a new structure. If screws are staining the beam, we can swap them while we are already on site.

Screen Replacement

Single panel screen repair is common after pets and patio furniture. We match color and weave when the rest of the cage is still serviceable. When half the roof is chalked, we talk through a full rescreen so you are not paying for overlapping visits. Screen replacement options cover both.

Lanai Screen Repair

West Bradenton and Bayshore Gardens lanais see more foot traffic than a backyard cage. Lanai screen repair usually starts at the door wall and the first course above the slab. We reset spline and, if the closer is tired, replace it so bugs stop walking in every evening.

Full Rescreening

Pool cage rescreening is the right call when the mesh is uniformly faded or you can poke a finger through several bays. A full rescreen lets us change mesh type in one pass. Screen enclosure repair of this scale is still cheaper than replacing the aluminum if the frame is sound.

Screen Materials

18/14 is the default for airflow. No-see-um screen is the usual upgrade near Palma Sola and other low, wet lots. Pet screen belongs on the lower walls of family lanais, not automatically on the entire roof. If paint is peeling or beams are oxidized, lanai restoration before new mesh gives the enclosure another decade.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you match my existing Bradenton screen?

Usually, if we can identify the weave. Older charcoal 18/14 is straightforward. If the cage mixed products over the years, we will show you the closest match before we start.

Do you handle small repairs after storms?

Yes. A few torn bays after a Manatee County storm are a normal call. We patch what failed and flag any fasteners that should be upgraded while we are there.
