Quick tips (Venice)

What screen is best for bugs in Venice?

If no-see-ums are an issue, a tighter mesh like 20/20 or upgraded polyester options can help. We’ll recommend the best fit for your airflow and protection goals.

Should I repair a few panels or rescreen everything?

If multiple panels are brittle or the screen is older (often 7–10 years), a full rescreen is usually the best long-term value. If damage is isolated, repairs are typically enough.
