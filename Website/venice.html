Pool Cage Repair

Gulf-side cages lose tension first on roof bays and on the wall that faces weather. Pool cage repair in Venice is often a handful of those panels plus hardware that has seized in the salt. If the aluminum is still straight, we keep it.

Screen Replacement

Single panel screen repair is enough when a chair or a falling frond punched one bay. We tension the new panel to the existing cage. When the whole roof looks like tissue, pool cage rescreening in one visit is cleaner. How we rescreen pool cages covers both scales.

Lanai Screen Repair

Venice lanais get evening use. If no-see-ums are coming through older 18/14, lanai screen repair may mean upgrading the walls you actually sit behind—not the entire roof. We will say which walls matter.

Full Rescreening

A full rescreen is the right move when spline is popping around the cage or the mesh color no longer matches from old patches. Screen enclosure repair of this type also lets us reset doors so they close against the new mesh.

Screen Materials

No-see-um (20/20) is the Venice conversation more often than inland. Pet screen still belongs on the kick course if dogs use the patio. 18/14 remains fine for roofs that only need airflow. Chalked frames should be washed, fastened, and painted before new mesh or the stains come back through.

Frequently Asked Questions

What screen is best for bugs in Venice?

If no-see-ums are the issue, 20/20 on the walls you sit behind is the usual fix. Roof 18/14 can stay if airflow matters more up there. We will not wrap the entire cage in 20/20 just because it is coastal.

Should I repair a few panels or rescreen everything?

Repair the torn bays if the rest still has stretch. Rescreen when patches already cover most of the roof or the mesh tears when you touch it.

Do you work while we are up north for the summer?

Yes, if someone can provide access. Send photos first so the scope is clear before we schedule.
