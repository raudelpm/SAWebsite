Frequently Asked Questions

How long does screen replacement or rescreening take?

Screen replacement is normally done in one day. A full refurbishment typically takes about 3 to 4 days. New construction is usually a matter of 1 to 2 weeks. We'll confirm the timeline when we provide your free estimate.

Do I need a permit for a screen enclosure or pool cage?

Permits are not needed for screen installation, refurbishment, or repairs. For new construction, requirements can vary depending on location, size, and type of enclosure. We can advise you on what applies to your project.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes. We offer a one-year warranty on labor. We use materials that have at least a 7-year manufacturer warranty.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Sarasota, Manatee, and Charlotte counties, including Parrish, Ellenton, Bradenton, Sarasota, Gulf Gate, Osprey, Nokomis, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Englewood, and surrounding communities.
