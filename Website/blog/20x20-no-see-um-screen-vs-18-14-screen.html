20x20 No-See-Um Screen vs 18/14 Screen: What’s the Difference?

Updated Apr 14, 2026 • Pool cage & lanai screening in Florida

If you're planning to repair or replace your pool cage or lanai screen in Florida, you've probably come across two common options: 18/14 standard screen and 20x20 no-see-um screen.

At first glance they may look similar, but the difference can significantly impact comfort—especially in areas like Sarasota and Bradenton where insects can be a real issue.

What do 18/14 and 20x20 mean?

These numbers refer to the number of mesh strands per inch:

  • 18/14 screen = 18 vertical strands and 14 horizontal strands
  • 20x20 screen = 20 vertical strands and 20 horizontal strands

Rule of thumb: the higher the number, the tighter the mesh.

18/14 screen (standard)

This is the most commonly used screen for pool enclosures and lanais.

Pros

  • Better airflow
  • Clearer visibility
  • More affordable

Cons

  • Does not block very small insects
  • No-see-ums can pass through

Best for: homeowners who want maximum airflow and don’t deal with heavy insect problems.

20x20 no-see-um screen

This screen is designed to block very small insects.

Pros

  • Keeps out no-see-ums, gnats, and small mosquitoes
  • Great for homes near water, lakes, or wooded areas

Cons

  • Reduced airflow vs 18/14
  • Slightly less visibility
  • More expensive

Best for: homeowners dealing with biting insects or living in high-bug areas.

Key differences at a glance

Feature 18/14 Screen 20x20 No-See-Um Screen
Airflow High Medium
Visibility High Medium
Bug protection Low–Medium High
Price Lower Higher

Which one should you choose?

It depends on your situation:

  • Choose 18/14 if you want better airflow and visibility.
  • Choose 20x20 no-see-um if bugs are a major problem.

In Florida—especially in humid areas—many homeowners upgrade to 20x20 for better comfort, even if it costs a bit more.

Final thoughts

Both options are solid. The right choice comes down to your environment and priorities. If you’re unsure, a professional can help you choose the best screen based on your location and how you use your space.
