Different Types of Pool Cage Screens Explained (And Which One Is Best)

Updated Apr 24, 2026 • Pool cage screening in Florida

Introduction

Not all pool cage screens are the same. Choosing the right one can make a big difference in durability, visibility, and protection from insects and heat.

Here’s a simple breakdown of the most common screen options in Florida.

1. 18/14 standard screen

This is the most common and affordable option.

Best for:

  • General use
  • Good airflow and visibility

Pros:

  • Budget-friendly
  • Easy to install

Cons:

  • Less protection against tiny insects

2. 20/20 no-see-um screen

A tighter mesh designed to keep out small bugs.

Best for:

  • Areas with mosquitoes and no-see-ums

Pros:

  • Better insect protection
  • Still decent airflow

Cons:

  • Slightly reduced airflow compared to 18/14

3. Polyester screen (no-see-um upgrade)

A stronger version of no-see-um screen.

Best for:

  • Homeowners looking for durability + bug protection

Pros:

  • More resistant to tearing
  • Longer lifespan

4. Pet screen (14/10)

Thicker and stronger mesh designed for pets.

Best for:

  • Homes with dogs or cats

Pros:

  • Tear-resistant
  • Stronger than standard screen

Cons:

  • Less visibility

5. Super screen

Premium screen with high durability and extended lifespan.

Best for:

  • Long-term investment

Pros:

  • Extremely strong
  • Can last longer than standard screens

6. Florida glass (privacy screen)

A laminated screen used mainly on lower panels.

Best for:

  • Privacy
  • Blocking debris and splash

7. Sun shade 40/40 screen

Designed to reduce sunlight and heat.

Best for:

  • Hot areas with direct sun exposure

Pros:

  • Reduces glare
  • Keeps area cooler

Which one should you choose?

It depends on your needs:

  • Budget option: 18/14
  • Bug protection: 20/20 or polyester
  • Durability: super screen or pet screen
  • Privacy: Florida glass
  • Heat reduction: sun shade

Conclusion

Choosing the right screen can improve comfort, durability, and overall experience in your pool area.
