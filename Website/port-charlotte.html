Port Charlotte is in Charlotte County, still on our regular route from Nokomis. After named storms, many calls are the same: roof mesh blown out, door off the closer, kick panel rusted. SCREEN ARMORS treats those as repair jobs unless the aluminum is bent.

Pool Cage Repair

Canal-front cages take more wind. Pool cage repair here is often several roof panels plus a door that no longer squares. We restretch mesh, reset hardware, and look at screws that have started to stain the beam.

Screen Replacement

Single panel screen repair still makes sense when one wall took debris. If patches already checkerboard the roof, a full rescreen is cleaner. Screen repair and rescreening are quoted as one scope so you are not surprised on site.

Lanai Screen Repair

Lanai screen repair in Port Charlotte often follows water and pets at the slab. We replace the lower course, check the threshold, and can install pet screen where claws hit every day—without wrapping the roof in heavy mesh.

Full Rescreening

Pool cage rescreening after a rough season lets us start clean: new spline, consistent color, doors that seal. Screen enclosure repair of this size is still a fraction of a new cage when the structure is true.

Screen Materials

18/14 for everyday airflow. No-see-um near water. Pet screen on kick panels. If the frame is chalked from years of Charlotte County sun, pool cage restoration (clean, stainless screws, paint) should happen before new mesh.
