Complete enclosure refurbishing & painting

From tired frame to like-new finish—one coordinated crew, end to end.

Serving Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, and Port Charlotte. See our full coverage list on Areas We Serve.

Bring aging pool cages and lanais back to life: frame repairs, rescreening, hardware updates, and full cosmetic restoration. SCREEN ARMORS plans each project around your goals so you get lasting performance—not a quick cover-up.

Inspection, repairs, pressure wash, marine-grade fasteners, masking, Sherwin-Williams DTM paint on aluminum, new door hardware and cables, then fresh screen in the material you choose. One thorough process, documented quality.

Our restoration process

  • Remove old screens; pressure wash the aluminum frame
  • Replace screws with 304 marine-grade stainless
  • Sand rust and rough spots; mask doors & non-paint surfaces
  • Spray Sherwin-Williams DTM for a durable metal finish
  • New door hardware & tension cables; full rescreen to spec
  • Final walkthrough so everything looks—and operates—like new

Benefits & add-on repairs

  • Often more cost-effective than full replacement
  • Extends enclosure life and restores curb appeal
  • Frame repairs for rusted or weakened members
  • Targeted screen replacement with premium materials
  • Hardware upgrades: hinges, fasteners, smooth door feel
  • Structural reinforcements where you need extra strength
