Pool Cage Repair

Builder cages in Lakewood Ranch fail at doors, roof corners, and the first course kids and dogs use. Pool cage repair is those panels and hardware—not a new structure. We keep the cage looking like the rest of the street, which matters on tightly spaced lots.

Screen Replacement

Single panel screen repair is the typical HOA-friendly fix: one torn bay, same charcoal weave, same day when we can. When the original mesh is uniformly faded, a full rescreen keeps the color even from the pool to the roof. Screen repair for pool cages covers both.

Lanai Screen Repair

Lakewood Ranch lanais are indoor-outdoor rooms. Lanai screen repair usually starts at the slider wall and the kick course. Pet screen on those lower bays is the usual family request; we leave the roof on standard mesh unless you want otherwise.

Full Rescreening

Pool cage rescreening here is often about appearance as much as holes: mismatched patches from prior vendors show from the sidewalk. A full rescreen resets spline and color in one pass. Screen enclosure repair of this type still costs less than replacing a sound builder frame.

Screen Materials

Most Lakewood Ranch cages shipped with 18/14 charcoal. No-see-um is an upgrade for conservation-edge lots, not a requirement on every street. Pet screen belongs where paws hit. If the aluminum is chalked, restoration and new mesh together look like a new cage without the teardown.
