Upgrade your outdoor space with a professionally built aluminum screen enclosure. At SCREEN ARMORS, we specialize in custom screen enclosures for pools, patios, and lanais, providing long-lasting protection while enhancing the look and value of your home.

Our aluminum enclosures are engineered to withstand Florida’s weather—strong sun, heavy rain, and humidity. Whether you need a brand-new enclosure or want to upgrade your existing structure, we deliver clean, precise, high-quality results every time.

What we offer

  • Custom aluminum screen enclosure design & installation
  • Patio & lanai enclosures
  • Structural repairs and upgrades
  • Screen replacement and rescreening
  • Hardware replacement (screws, fasteners, framing components)

Benefits of aluminum enclosures

  • Protects against insects, debris, and harsh sunlight

  • Adds value and curb appeal to your property
