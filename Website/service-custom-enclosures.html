Composite Roof Screen Enclosure

A solid roof over screened walls. Shade from the sun, cover from the rain, and airflow through the screens.

Composite roof

A Dry, Shaded Porch That Still Feels Open

A composite roof screen enclosure gives you a real roof overhead and screens around the sides.

The roof is solid composite paneling, not screen. It blocks the midday sun and keeps rain off the porch, so the space stays usable on a hot afternoon and during a passing Florida storm.

The walls stay screened. Air still moves through, insects stay outside, and you keep the open feel of a porch instead of closing the room in with windows.

The aluminum frame is measured to your porch and tied into the house, with a screen door for everyday access. It is a good fit for a front porch, back porch, or lanai where a screen roof would let in too much heat and rain.

What You Get

  • Solid Composite Roof Shade and rain cover overhead. There is no roof screen to tear in wind or sun.
  • Screened Walls Airflow and insect protection around the porch, with a clear view out.
  • Custom Aluminum Frame Built to the footprint of your porch and finished to match the home.
  • Usable In Sun And Rain A cooler, drier outdoor room than an all-screen roof on a Florida afternoon.
  • Screen Door Included Everyday access without leaving the porch open to insects.
  • Marine Grade 304 Stainless Steel Screws We fasten this porch enclosure with Marine Grade 304 Stainless Steel Screws.

Custom Enclosure Questions

Straight answers about front porch entries, composite roof enclosures, and what to expect from Screen Armors.

What is a custom screen enclosure?

It is an aluminum frame with screen, built to the openings on your porch. On this page: a front porch entry, a composite roof screen enclosure, and a covered patio.

What is the difference between a front porch entry and a composite roof?

A front porch entry screens the entrance and can include a decorative aluminum screen door. A composite roof enclosure has a solid composite roof for shade and rain, with screened walls so air still moves through.

Will a composite roof keep the porch dry?

Yes. The roof is solid composite paneling, not screen, so rain stays off the porch. The walls stay screened, which keeps insects out and lets air through.

Can I choose the door style and color?

Yes. Decorative screen doors are available in different patterns, finishes, and colors so the door can match your home and the aluminum around it.

Do I need a permit?

No. You do not need a permit for these screen enclosures.

How long does a custom enclosure take?

It depends on the size, but most enclosures can be built in one day. The wait before we start usually comes down to HOA approval and sending the documents they ask for. We confirm the timeline with your free estimate.

What warranty is included?

Every custom enclosure includes a 3-year warranty on our workmanship. The manufacturer warranty varies depending on the material.

What maintenance does it need?

Aluminum framing is low maintenance. Rinse the screens now and then, and call us if a panel tears or a fastener loosens. A composite roof does not have a screen roof to replace.

Where do you build custom enclosures?

We build across Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Lakewood Ranch. See the full list on Areas We Serve.

How do I get a price?

The price depends on the porch size, whether you want a composite roof, and the door style. Request a free estimate or call (941) 404-9699 and we will price the enclosure for your home.
