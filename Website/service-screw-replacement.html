Pool cage screw replacement

Small hardware, big impact—restore strength before rust spreads.

Protect your enclosure with professional screw replacement. Fasteners rust and weaken from Florida humidity, rain, and salt air—SCREEN ARMORS swaps failing hardware for corrosion-resistant screws so your cage stays tight and safe.

We inspect every connection, remove corroded screws, install premium stainless or coated fasteners, reinforce joints, and verify the structure end-to-end. Don’t wait for small issues to become expensive structural repairs.

Why it matters

  • Helps prevent structural weakening and failure
  • Stops rust stains and corrosion from spreading
  • Improves overall strength and stability
  • Better storm readiness ahead of hurricane season
  • Extends the life of the entire enclosure

Signs & our process

  • Rusted, missing, or spinning screws; loose frame sections
  • Brown rust streaks or aging original hardware
  • Process: full inspection → careful removal → rust-resistant upgrade
  • Reinforced connection points and final stability check
  • Materials chosen for coastal Florida exposure
