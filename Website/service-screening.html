Materials

Screen Meshes We Offer

Choose the mesh for the job: everyday insects, no-see-ums, pets, privacy, shade, or a stronger open view.

★ Most popular

18/14 Standard Screen

The standard fiberglass screen for pool cages and patio enclosures. It blocks most insects, still lets air through, and has the minimum visibility restriction.

★ Most popular

20/20 No-See-Um Screen

Fiberglass no-see-um screen for the smallest flying insects, including no-see-ums, gnats, sand flies, and other tiny pests.

★ Ideal for Clearview

16/14 & 17/20 Polyester Screen

Heavy-duty polyester in two weaves. 16/14 is the more open mesh. 17/20 is the tighter weave for extra strength and tear resistance, while still allowing airflow and visibility. Ideal for pool cages and lanais that need a stronger, longer-lasting screen.

PetScreen

Heavy-duty polyester, stronger and thicker than standard screen, so claws are less likely to tear it. Used on doors, lower panels, and high-traffic areas. This is the 14/10 pet screen in our product options.

Privacy option

Florida Glass

Vinyl-laminated fiberglass that creates an opaque privacy panel. On lower panels it blocks wind, rain, dirt, and debris as well as the view.

Shade option

40/40 Shade Screen

Premium shade screen designed to reduce sunlight, heat, and glare while providing added daytime privacy. Its tightly woven mesh allows airflow while creating a cooler, more comfortable outdoor space. Ideal for pool cages, lanais, and patios exposed to direct sunlight.

Super Screen is the stronger mesh when you want a more open view.

Screen Repair and Rescreening Questions

Can you replace just one screen panel?

Yes. Pool screen replacement on one wall, door, or roof panel leaves the surrounding mesh in place. See single panel replacement.

When should I choose a full rescreen instead of repairs?

Use a full rescreen when patches and worn mesh are spread around the enclosure. If the rest of the screen is still sound, replace one panel or several panels only.

How much does screen repair or rescreening cost?

Price depends on the number of panels, the mesh, and whether the job is a repair or a full rescreen. Small repairs are usually quoted from photos. A full rescreen is priced through a free estimate.

Can I receive a repair quote by texting photos?

Yes, for most small repairs. Text a close-up, a wider photo of the area, and your city to (941) 404-9699. Larger jobs may need a visit. Full rescreening uses the estimate form.

Do I need to be home during the repair?

Not when we can reach the enclosure from outside. Eligible repairs can be finished without you there, and you can pay online. Not every repair qualifies.

What types of screen materials are available?

18/14 standard screen, 20/20 no-see-um mesh, 16/14 and 17/20 heavy-duty polyester screen, PetScreen, Florida Glass, 40/40 Shade Screen, and Super Screen. Compare them in screen mesh.

Can you match my existing screen?

We match weave and color as closely as we can. Screen that has faded in the sun can still look different next to a new panel.

Do you repair damaged aluminum framing?

This page covers screen replacement. If the frame or other parts need work, we point it out and quote that separately. Cage refurbishing covers frame work beyond new screen.

What warranty is included?

Panel replacement and full rescreening include a 2-year warranty on our workmanship. The screen we install carries a manufacturer warranty starting at 5 years.

Which areas do you serve?

Sarasota, Manatee, and Charlotte counties, including Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Lakewood Ranch. See the full list on Areas We Serve.
