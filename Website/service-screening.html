03 · Full enclosure

Full Pool Cage & Lanai Rescreening

Full rescreen

If several areas of your enclosure have brittle mesh, recurring tears, or older repairs, replacing all the screen at once can give the enclosure a consistent finish.

We remove the existing mesh and spline, then install new screen across the enclosure, including the walls and roof. The existing aluminum frame stays in place. If we find damage to the frame or other components, we’ll explain what needs attention and quote that work separately.

Choose standard 18/14 mesh for everyday insect protection or 20/20 no-see-um mesh for smaller insects. We can also install PetScreen on doors or lower panels that get extra wear, or Florida Glass on select lower panels for added privacy.

Materials

Screen Mesh

The new screen can match the enclosure, or change where you want different insect protection, pet resistance, or privacy. These are the meshes we install on repairs and rescreens.

★ Most popular

18/14 Standard Screen

The standard polyester screen for patio and pool enclosures. Designed to block most insects and unwanted pests while still allowing fresh, cooling airflow to pass through.

★ Most popular

20/20 No-See-Um Screen

Known as polyester no-see-um screen, designed to keep out even the smallest flying insects such as no-see-ums, gnats, sand flies, and other tiny pests while maintaining airflow.

PetScreen

Heavy-duty polyester pet screen designed to resist damage from pets. Stronger and thicker than standard screen, it helps prevent tears from claws while still allowing airflow. Ideal for doors, lower panels, and high-traffic areas.

Florida Glass

Heavy-duty Florida Glass privacy screen, made from vinyl-laminated fiberglass for added durability and privacy. Designed to block wind, rain, dirt, and debris while creating an opaque barrier—ideal for lower panels of pool and patio enclosures.

Sunshade 40/40 is available for more shade and less heat. Super Screen is available for a more open view with extra strength. PetScreen on this page is the 14/10 pet screen listed in our product options.

Screen Repair and Rescreening Questions

Answers about one panel, a full rescreen, texting photos, and what Screen Armors covers.

Do you offer full pool cage and lanai rescreening?

Yes. Full rescreening replaces all the existing mesh across the enclosure, including the walls and roof. The aluminum frame stays in place. If the frame or other components need work, that is quoted separately. Get a free full rescreen estimate.

When should I replace a few panels or rescreen the entire enclosure?

Replace the damaged panels when the rest of the mesh is still in good condition. That can be one panel or several panels, such as the lower panels, the panels around a door, or several roof panels. Rescreen the enclosure when several areas have brittle mesh, recurring tears, or older repairs and you want a consistent finish.

Can you repair just one panel?

Yes. We replace one damaged wall, door, or roof panel and leave the rest of the screen in place. See single panel replacement.

How does Text Photos for a Repair Quote work?

Text a close-up, a wider photo of the affected area, and your city to (941) 404-9699. We can quote most small repairs from those photos. Larger jobs may need an on-site visit. For a full rescreen, use the estimate form.

How much does screen repair or rescreening cost?

The price depends on how many panels, the mesh, and whether the job is a repair or a full rescreen. Small repairs can usually be quoted from photos. A full rescreen is priced from a free estimate. Request a free estimate or text photos.

Can you match my existing screen?

We match the weave and color as closely as we can. Sun-faded screen next to a new panel can look different. Replacing all the mesh keeps the enclosure consistent.

What screen types can you install?

18/14 polyester for everyday insect protection, 20/20 no-see-um mesh for smaller insects, PetScreen (14/10 pet screen) for doors and lower panels, and Florida Glass for privacy on select lower panels. Sunshade 40/40 and Super Screen are also available. Compare them in screen mesh.

Do you offer pet screen?

Yes. PetScreen can go on doors or lower panels that get extra wear. Other panels can stay standard 18/14.

Do I need to be home?

Not if we can reach the enclosure. Most of the work is done from outside the house.

What if it rains?

Light rain usually does not stop a panel repair. A larger rescreen may wait for a clearer day.

Do you fix the frame, or only the screen?

We replace the screen. If we find damage to the frame or other components, we explain what needs attention and quote that work separately. Cage refurbishing is available when the frame needs more than screen.

Do I need a permit?

Permits are not needed for screen repair or rescreening. New enclosure construction is a different project, and permit rules for that work can vary.

What warranty is included?

Screen repair and full rescreening include the same 2-year warranty on our workmanship. The materials we install carry at least a 7-year manufacturer warranty.

Where do you repair and rescreen?

Sarasota, Manatee, and Charlotte counties, including Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Lakewood Ranch. See the full list on Areas We Serve.
