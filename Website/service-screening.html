How we handle screen repair

One service line—clear options—so homeowners know they're not signing up for more than they need.

  • Small screen repair

    Localized tears, rub holes, or storm damage in one area. We match mesh type and color where possible and restore integrity without redoing the whole cage.

  • Panel replacement

    Swap out individual bays or panels when the spline, mesh, or a section of frame-adjacent screen has failed—ideal when the rest of the enclosure is still in good shape.

  • Partial rescreen

    Refresh a wall, roof section, or high-wear side of a pool cage or lanai. You get a like-new feel in the zones that matter most, with scope scaled to your budget.

Materials

Screen Mesh Types

Choose the right mesh for airflow, durability, privacy, and insect protection.

★ Most popular

18/14 Standard Screen

The standard polyester screen for patio and pool enclosures. Designed to block most insects and unwanted pests while still allowing fresh, cooling airflow to pass through.

★ Most popular

20/20 No-See-Um Screen

Known as polyester no-see-um screen, designed to keep out even the smallest flying insects such as no-see-ums, gnats, sand flies, and other tiny pests while maintaining airflow.

PetScreen

Heavy-duty polyester pet screen designed to resist damage from pets. Stronger and thicker than standard screen, it helps prevent tears from claws while still allowing airflow. Ideal for doors, lower panels, and high-traffic areas.

Florida Glass

Heavy-duty Florida Glass privacy screen, made from vinyl-laminated fiberglass for added durability and privacy. Designed to block wind, rain, dirt, and debris while creating an opaque barrier—ideal for lower panels of pool and patio enclosures.
