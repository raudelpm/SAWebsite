01 · One bay

Single Panel Repair

Single panel

One Tear Does Not Mean A New Cage

A chair, a pet, a falling branch, or a storm often takes out one bay. That bay is the job.

We remove the damaged screen, set new mesh in the opening, and lock it with fresh spline so the panel stays tight. The rest of the enclosure stays in place.

We match the weave and color as closely as we can. Sun-faded screen next to a new panel can look a little different. If the surrounding mesh is already failing, we will tell you before we patch one hole in a worn wall.

A Single Panel Covers

  • A Hole Or A Tear One opening on a wall, a door, or the roof. Isolated damage stays an isolated repair.
  • Pet Damage Claws on a door or the lower course. We can step that panel up to pet screen so it holds up better.
  • Screen Pulling Out Spline that has let go along the frame. New spline and new screen put the panel back under tension.
  • Priced From A Photo Most one-panel jobs can be priced from Send a Picture, Get a Repair without a visit first.

03 · Wall or roof

A Whole Section

Wall, side, or roof

When Patching Would Only Buy A Season

A whole wall, one side of the cage, or the roof screen, replaced together so the mesh matches and the tension is even.

This is the move when the screen is chalky, brittle, or full of small holes. New panels next to 15-year-old mesh never quite blend, and the old screen is usually next to fail.

Most section rescreens are finished in one day. If the aluminum is bent, the fasteners are failing, or the frame needs more than screen, we will say that before the work starts. Frame repairs and cage refurbishing are separate from a rescreen.

You can stay with standard 18/14, move a wall to 20/20 no-see-um, or put Florida Glass on the lower panels for privacy. The choices are on the mesh section below.

A Section Rescreen Is For

  • One Wall Or One Side Every bay on that face, so the color and the tension match from post to post.
  • The Roof Overhead screen that has torn, sagged, or chalked in the sun. Roof work is priced for the access it takes.
  • Mesh That Has Aged Out Screen that powders when you touch it, or a wall where half the bays are already patched.
  • A Clear Recommendation If a single panel is the honest fix, that is what we quote. A full section is for screen that will not hold.

Materials

Screen Mesh For The Repair

The new panel can match what is on the cage, or step up where the wear is. Airflow, pets, privacy, and the smallest insects are the usual reasons to change mesh.

★ Most popular

18/14 Standard Screen

The standard polyester screen for patio and pool enclosures. Designed to block most insects and unwanted pests while still allowing fresh, cooling airflow to pass through.

★ Most popular

20/20 No-See-Um Screen

Known as polyester no-see-um screen, designed to keep out even the smallest flying insects such as no-see-ums, gnats, sand flies, and other tiny pests while maintaining airflow.

PetScreen

Heavy-duty polyester pet screen designed to resist damage from pets. Stronger and thicker than standard screen, it helps prevent tears from claws while still allowing airflow. Ideal for doors, lower panels, and high-traffic areas.

Florida Glass

Heavy-duty Florida Glass privacy screen, made from vinyl-laminated fiberglass for added durability and privacy. Designed to block wind, rain, dirt, and debris while creating an opaque barrier—ideal for lower panels of pool and patio enclosures.

Sunshade 40/40 is available when a wall needs more shade and less heat. Super Screen is available when you want a more open view with extra strength. We will say which mesh fits the repair when we price it.

Screen Repair Questions

Straight answers about one panel, a whole wall, texting a photo, and what to expect from Screen Armors.

Can you repair just one panel?

Yes. A single torn bay, a door, or one roof panel is a normal job. A full rescreen is the right call only when many areas are damaged or the screen is old and worn out. See single panel repair.

How does Send a Picture, Get a Repair work?

Text photos to (941) 404-9699. A close-up of the tear plus a wider shot of that wall, door, or roof is usually enough to price a small repair. Add your city and about how many panels look the same.

How much does screen repair cost?

It depends on how many panels, the mesh, and whether the repair is a wall, a door, or the roof. Small jobs can be priced from photos. Larger ones get a free estimate with a firm price before any work starts. Request an estimate or send a picture.

Can you match my existing screen?

We match the weave and color as closely as we can. Sun-faded screen next to a new panel can look a little different. If the whole wall is faded, a section rescreen keeps the color even.

What screen types can you install?

18/14 standard polyester, 20/20 no-see-um, pet screen, and Florida Glass are the usual choices. Sunshade 40/40 and Super Screen are available when the repair calls for more shade or a stronger, more open view. Compare them in screen mesh.

Do you offer pet screen?

Yes. Heavier pet screen goes on doors, the lower course, and anywhere a dog or cat leans on the mesh. The panels above can stay standard if they are still in good shape.

How long does a repair take?

Most screen replacement is done in one day. Smaller repairs are often the same day or the next day, depending on the schedule. We confirm the day when we send the price.

Do I need to be home?

Not if we can reach the enclosure. Most repairs are finished from the outside of the house.

What if it rains?

Light rain usually does not stop a repair. A large rescreen may move to a clearer day so the screen goes in tight.

Do you fix the frame, or only the screen?

We replace screen and handle minor frame repairs. If the aluminum is bent, rusted through, or the cage needs more than new mesh, we will say that before the work starts. Cage refurbishing is a separate service.

Do I need a permit?

Permits are not needed for screen repairs.

What warranty is included?

Screen repair includes a 2-year warranty on our workmanship. The screen materials we install carry at least a 7-year manufacturer warranty.

How long will the new screen last?

Most screen lasts about 7 to 10 years, depending on sun, wind, and how the cage is used.

Where do you repair screens?

We repair screens across Sarasota, Bradenton, Venice, North Port, Port Charlotte, and Lakewood Ranch. See the full list on Areas We Serve.
